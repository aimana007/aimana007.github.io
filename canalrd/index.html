Buy It Now

Direct path: Buy before auction.

Before this reaches the public auction, there's a private option to buy it directly from the owner. This is not a listing and not yet a binding offer. It's the proposed shape of a deal, open to a serious buyer who moves before the redemption period ends.

  • 1. Redeem the tax debt. Buyer pays the outstanding tax balance (~$25,000, the same figure shown in The Numbers) directly to Orleans County. This redeems the property and stops the foreclosure.
  • 2. Sign a private purchase contract. Buyer and owner sign a contract for the tax balance amount plus $65,000 additional (negotiable): $10,000 due at signing, $55,000 due at closing/title transfer (see step 5 closing happens once the owner receives letters testamentary).
  • 3. Immediate access. Buyer gets immediate access to the second house (15955 Canal Rd) and the detached garage/workshop.
  • 4. Owner stays through year-end. The owner continues living in the main house (15943 Canal Rd) through the end of the year, then vacates.
  • 5. Deed transfers once legal authority is finalized by surrogates court. The owner is currently the outgoing heir, not yet the estate's court-appointed executor. Closing: the deed transfer and the buyer's final $55,000 payment happens once the owner receives letters testamentary (formal legal authority to convey the property). Timing is dependant on court, not guaranteed by year-end.
Only available before the redemption period ends. The redemption period ends Wednesday, August 26, 2026, 11:30 AM. After that date, this option goes away and the property proceeds to the public tax foreclosure auction described below.

This is a private, direct-from-owner arrangement, not a licensed real estate transaction, and not yet a signed or binding offer, terms above are proposed and may change before a contract is signed. Deed transfer (and the final $55,000 payment) is contingent on the owner receiving letters testamentary, which has not happened yet and is not guaranteed on any particular timeline. Any buyer should involve their own real estate attorney before signing anything or sending money.

The Story

Two houses, one pond, and the math behind the upside

This was my mother's property. After she passed, the tax debt came to me as her heir, and I'm still waiting on the probate court to formally recognize me before I can transfer the deed outright. That's the real reason some of the dates and terms on this page are still placeholders, not disorganization, just where things actually stand.

  • Two legal dwellings, one parcel. The main house (15943 Canal Road, 2 bed / 1 bath, 1,128 sq ft, built 1954) is livable now. The second house (15955 Canal Road, 2 bed / 1 bath, 792 sq ft, built 1989) needs some work. They're separate buildings, not one house split in two.
  • Water and open land. A private pond sits between the two houses. The NYS Erie Canal and a quarry are just beyond the property line.
  • Detached garage and workshop, already standing. ~1,548 sq ft with a separate workshop area, plus a gazebo and shed, nothing here needs to be built first.
  • Low opening bid. This is a tax foreclosure auction: the opening bid starts near the ~$25,000 tax debt, well under the 2026 total assessment of $125,900. Where that gap leaves room is on the right.
